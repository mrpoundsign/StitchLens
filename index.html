Free · Zero-Install · 100% Browser-Based

See your crochet patterns in 3D—stitch by stitch.

Paste standard US/UK crochet instructions or rough live-drafted notes to visualize how flat rows, magic-ring spirals, oval bases, and multi-piece plushies take shape in 3D before you pick up yarn and hook.

Launch StitchLens App → Explore 11 CC0 Starter Patterns

Live Scratchpad & Stitch-Count Checker

Draft patterns without spellcheck squiggles. Understands Word-style numbered lists (3. Chain, turn, 2 inc (4)), row ranges, omitted flat-row turns, # / // comments, and verifies stitch counts on every row.

Step-by-Step Hook Target Spotlight

Never guess where your hook goes next. Scrub stitch-by-stitch or row-by-row while a glowing target ring highlights the exact Front Loop (FLO), Back Loop (BLO), post, chain space, or underside chain.

Plush 3D Yarn & Diagram X-Ray Views

Toggle between tactile twisted-ply 3D yarn loops and a topological stitch-graph diagram color-coded by pattern yarn color, alternating rows, stitch height, or tension stress.

Plain-English Assembly & 3D Click-to-Pin

Assemble multi-part amigurumi and rolled flower bouquets using plain English (Attach Left Ear to left of Head at Rnd 4) or click any stitch directly on the 3D model to pin pieces together.
